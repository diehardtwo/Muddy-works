Where to ride around Hastings, Bexhill and Battle

  • Hastings to Bexhill seafront. A flat, traffic-free ride of about 5 km (3 miles) along the coast on National Cycle Route 2. Easy going for beginners and families, with sea views all the way.
  • Combe Valley Countryside Park. Sits between Hastings and Bexhill, with reedbeds, fen, riverside paths and a nature reserve. The Combe Valley Greenway runs through it, so you can loop out of the seafront for a bit of countryside.
  • Cooden Beach. The cycle route runs right by this gently sloping shingle beach, which makes it a good place to stop for a picnic.
  • Hastings Old Town loop. A flat loop of around 12 km (about 45 minutes) taking in the Old Town and the fishing net shops. Take your time and stop for fish and chips.
  • Battle Abbey and the battlefield. Make a day of it in Battle, where the 1066 battlefield and abbey are. Near Crowhurst you can also spot the old railway line with its brick arches.

Check your route before you set off, and keep an eye on the weather. The seafront can get breezy.
