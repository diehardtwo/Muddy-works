Gear and brake adjustment in Hastings, Bexhill and Battle

Gears skipping, or brakes squealing and spongy? A proper adjustment often sorts it without replacing anything.

What it costs

£15 for a gear adjustment or a brake adjustment on its own. Parts are extra, and I'll tell you the price before I fit anything.

Signs your gears need adjusting

  • The chain skips or jumps under pressure
  • It's slow or noisy to change gear
  • It won't settle into the gear you picked

Signs your brakes need adjusting

  • The levers pull close to the bars before they bite
  • Squealing, or a rub as you ride
  • You're stopping more slowly than you used to

What I do

On gears, I adjust the cable tension and indexing so every click lands in the right gear. On brakes, I align the pads and set the tension so they bite evenly and don't rub. Adjusting can't fix worn parts, so if something needs replacing, I'll say so.
