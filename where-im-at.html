Where I'm at right now

1 October 2026 · Adam

Right, first post. Muddyworks is brand new, so rather than pretend I've got it all figured out, I thought I'd be straight with you about where things really are.

What I'm doing right now

I'm just starting out, and I'm using this early bit to get properly good at the basics. That means simple servicing: gears, brakes and a proper grease up, plus a good look over the whole bike to spot anything that's about to give you grief.

I'd rather do the small jobs really well than take on something big and make a mess of it. Every bike I work on makes me quicker, tidier and better at spotting problems before they turn into expensive ones.

What's coming next

Once I've nailed the basics, I want to add more to the list:

  • Wheel truing: getting rid of that wobble.
  • Full wheel rebuilds: when a wheel needs more than a tweak.
  • Upgrades: making the bike you've got better than the one you bought.
  • Suspension servicing: because forks that feel like a pogo stick help nobody.

None of that is on offer yet. When it is, I'll say so right here.

Bike hire in Hastings and Bexhill

Not live yet

The big plan is to hire bikes out across Hastings and Bexhill. Here's how I want it to work:

  1. You book online.
  2. You tell me where you want the bike, and I drop it off at a spot of your choice.
  3. You ride it, then leave it wherever suits you when you're done.
  4. I come and pick it up later.

No queues, no shop to find and no racing back before closing time.
