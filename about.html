About Muddyworks

Hello, I'm Adam. Muddyworks is my bike workshop in Hastings, built on a lifetime of riding and a career in engineering.

A lifetime on bikes

My earliest bike memories are of riding my cousin's Grifter and my mum's shopper. I spent my early years on BMXs, building ramps, before moving on to mountain biking.

Like many riders, I worked my way through the budget brands first: Emmelle, Muddy Fox and Diamondback. Then I was lucky enough to get my hands on an Orange Clockwork and an Orange O2.

The bikes along the way

  • Cousin's Grifter and mum's shopperFirst rides
  • BMXsEarly years
  • Emmelle, Muddy Fox, DiamondbackFirst mountain bikes
  • Orange Clockwork and Orange O2Dream come true
  • Specialized Stumpjumper and Genesis CoreLater years
  • Scott AspectRiding now

Every rider has a wish list. Mine was a Redneck stem, a Klein Adroit Pro, RockShox SID forks and a full XTR groupset. The Redneck stem was the only one I ever got to own, on a custom sprayed BMX.

Engineering background

I've tinkered with bikes all my life. I also spent many years as a sheet metal engineer and welder, and more recently I've applied those engineering skills to maintaining water levels for the Environment Agency.

Why Muddyworks

Now feels like the right time to put my love of bikes and my engineering skills to work doing what I enjoy most: working with bikes. If yours needs attention, I'd be glad to help.
